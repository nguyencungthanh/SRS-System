# Wear leveling (new NFR) - month-end trip counter spread

Generated by `python -m optimization.wear` in 735 s with the real modules.

## Rule checked (DECISIONS D-71..D-79)

- Trip counter = floors travelled by each car; compared cars: P1, P2, P3, P4 (G1 left out, D-73).
- Spread = (max - min) / max at every month end; limit 5 % (D-76; the e-mail asked for 2 %, so the 2 % column is shown too).
- Counters accumulate and are reset every 6 months (D-76, D-77).
- A car in maintenance during a month is left out of that month (D-75).
- Dispatch balancing (D-78): a car's pickup ETA gets 0.2 s per floor it has travelled beyond the least-worn candidate, at most 8 s.

**Months are shortened to 2 h of simulated traffic** (one passenger every 12 s on average). A real month is 30 days. A shorter month is the harder case for a balancing dispatcher: the totals are smaller while the gap it leaves stays about the same.
